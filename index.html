About TechBridge

TechBridge is an internship and learning initiative built to help aspiring professionals bridge the gap between what they've learned and what the real world actually demands.

Rather than relying on theory alone, the initiative is built around practical tasks, guided projects, and hands on activities. Participants work through real world style assignments designed to develop relevant technical skills, build confidence, and gain genuine, applicable experience.

Approach Practical, project-based learning
Format Guided tasks & real world style projects
Focus Skill development & hands on experience

Programs

Two structured tracks for aspiring professionals.

Data Analytics

A track focused on developing practical data analytics skills through guided tasks and hands on activities, helping participants build a working understanding of how data-driven work is done in practice.

Web Development

A track focused on developing practical web development skills through guided tasks and real world style projects, giving participants hands-on experience building for the web.

Internship

30 Day Practical Internship

TechBridge runs a 30 Day Practical Internship centered on project-based learning and real world style tasks. Participants work through structured assignments designed to mirror the kind of work they'll encounter in professional settings.

  • Successful participation and performance may qualify participants for a recommendation letter.
  • Successful completion may also qualify participants for completion documentation.
30 Days of practical, project based learning
